Refund Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

If ReadyGate Pro isn't right for you, you can ask for a full refund within 14 days of purchase.

How to ask

Email hello@readygate.dev with your order number, or contact Paddle through the link in your receipt. Paddle, our reseller and Merchant of Record, processes all refunds to your original payment method.

What happens after a refund

A refund (or a chargeback) ends your Pro license. Your access to the private repository is removed, and under the license you must stop using and delete the Software, including copies you downloaded.

After 14 days

Requests after 14 days aren't normally accepted. This doesn't affect your rights if the product is faulty or not as described, or any other rights under consumer law.

Free version

ReadyGate (free) costs nothing, so there's nothing to refund. You can try it before buying Pro.
