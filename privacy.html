Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

This policy covers readygate.dev and ReadyGate purchases. The data controller is Ayoub Ben Thabet (ReadyGate), Tunisia. Contact: hello@readygate.dev.

This website

The site uses no cookies or advertising. It counts visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which records page views without cookies, fingerprinting or storing your IP address. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log your IP address for security, under GitHub's privacy statement.

Purchases

Payments are processed by Paddle.com, our reseller and Merchant of Record. Paddle collects your payment and billing details under its own privacy notice; we never see your card details. From Paddle we receive your name, email address, country, order details and the GitHub username you entered.

What we use it for

  • Your GitHub username: to give that account access to the private Pro repository, and remove it after a refund.
  • Your email: to send access details, important updates about your purchase, and to answer you. No marketing without your consent.
  • Order details: accounting, tax and fraud prevention, as the law requires.

The legal basis is performing our contract with you and meeting legal obligations.

The plugin

ReadyGate has no servers and sends us nothing. It runs inside Claude Code on your machine; your code and tickets are handled by Claude Code under Anthropic's terms, and by the trackers you connect under their terms.

Sharing and retention

We don't sell your data. It is shared only with the services above (Paddle, GitHub) and with authorities when the law requires. Order records are kept as long as tax law requires; other data is deleted when no longer needed or when you ask.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, or object to its use, by emailing hello@readygate.dev. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.
