# Design System: ReadyGate

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Product, Shown Working"**

ReadyGate's site is the category-standard SaaS product page played straight at Linear and Cal.com craft: a near-white neutral ground, one deep teal ink, hairline borders, soft lifted surfaces and Inter at tight display tracking. Nothing on the page is decoration standing in for the product. The persuading is done by faithful renderings of ReadyGate at work: a Claude Code terminal pane with real commands and `file:line` citations, and a ticket on warm paper with BR/AC identifiers.

The palette is the icon's. Teal is the brand ink and the primary action; mint is the "ready / checked" signal; warm off-white paper exists only inside product renderings. A single orange state hue marks "decision needed" and "Blocked", the moment ReadyGate refuses to guess. Density is calm: generous 120px section rhythm, centered hero, two-column content grids that collapse to one column under 900px. Light and dark themes are both first-class; dark mode turns the ground deep teal-black and promotes mint to the brand role.

Motion is a single authored moment: the product window settles into place on load. Everything else is instantaneous or a short state transition.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Near-white neutral ground, deep teal as the only brand ink, mint as the ready signal.
- Product renderings (terminal, ticket paper) carry the visual interest; no illustrations, gradient blobs or icon-card grids.
- Hairline 1px borders plus soft, low-contrast offset shadows; 10-16px radii.
- Inter for everything readable, JetBrains Mono only for real commands, paths and `file:line` references.
- One orange state hue, reserved for "decision needed" and "Blocked".
- One entrance animation (the window settle), transform-only, off under reduced motion.

## Colors

A restrained neutral-teal system with one signal green and one state orange, all lifted from the product icon except the orange.

### Primary
- **Deep Gate Teal** (brand-teal): primary buttons, links, the Pro plan's ring, command names in lists, ticket IDs, and the closing CTA band. Hover deepens to brand-teal-hover. In dark mode the brand role becomes mint (#5ED6A0, hover #7FE2B6) with dark ink (#06251A) on it, because teal does not read on the teal-black ground.
- **On-Brand White** (on-brand): text on teal fills.

### Secondary
- **Ready Mint** (ready-mint): the "ready / checked" signal. Success output and command highlights in the terminal, the citation dot, text selection, and the dark-mode brand fill.
- **Mint Ink** (mint-ink) on **Mint Tint** (mint-tint): legible form of the signal on light surfaces: check icons, step numerals, feature icon tiles, the "One-time" pill, the "Ready for dev" tag, purchase confirmation. Dark mode: mint-ink #7FE2B6 on mint-tint #0F3328.

### Tertiary
- **Hold Orange** (hold-orange) on **Hold Tint** (hold-tint): the one state hue outside the icon palette. Used only for "decision needed" and "Blocked" states, plus invalid input and the purchase error message. Dark mode: #FB923C on #2A1A10. Inside terminal fragments the hold label uses **Hold Peach** (hold-term) over the hold wash (hold-term-wash) inside a hold hairline (hold-term-border).

### Neutral
- **Near-White Page** (page) and **Sunk Page** (page-sunk): page ground; sunk fills for window bars, table heads, inline code, command chips and nav hover. Dark: #071417 and #0B1D21.
- **Surface White** (surface): cards, plans, the window frame, secondary buttons. Dark: #0E2328.
- **Teal-Black Ink** (ink), **Slate Ink** (ink-2), **Muted Slate** (ink-3): headings and strong text; body and descriptions; meta, fine print, placeholders. Dark: #E8F0F1, #B3C4C7, #8BA0A4.
- **Hairline** (line) and **Strong Hairline** (line-strong): dividers and card borders; button, input and window borders. Dark: #18343A and #234850.
- **Warm Paper** (paper) with **Paper Ink** (paper-ink) and **Paper Muted** (paper-muted): the ticket surface inside product renderings only, with muted ink for file paths and section labels. The ticket's status tag sits on **Ticket Tag Mint** (ticket-tag-bg). Stays warm and light in both themes.
- **Terminal Night** (term), **Terminal Ink** (term-ink), **Terminal Muted** (term-muted), **Terminal Ref Blue** (term-ref): the Claude Code pane and output fragments; ref blue marks code references and paths. Dark: term #04100F.
- **Focus Teal** (focus): the 2px focus outline and input focus ring. Dark: #7FE2B6.

### Dark Theme
Dark mode (prefers-color-scheme: dark) re-maps the same roles onto the `dark-*` tokens: dark-page, dark-page-sunk, dark-surface, dark-ink, dark-ink-2, dark-ink-3, dark-line, dark-line-strong, dark-term, dark-mint-tint, dark-hold and dark-hold-tint. The brand role becomes dark-brand (mint) with dark-on-brand ink, and **Mint Light** (mint-light) serves as brand hover, mint-ink and focus. Dark-on-brand also colours selected text in both themes. Paper, terminal ink, terminal ref and hold peach do not change.

### Named Rules
**The Paper Stays In The Window Rule.** Warm paper (#F2EFE8) appears only as the surface of a rendered ticket or ticket fragment. The page ground is always the cool near-white (or teal-black in dark mode), never paper.

**The One State Hue Rule.** Orange means "a human decision is needed" and nothing else (plus its direct form-validation echo). It is never decoration, never a second accent, never a CTA.

**The Fixed Closing Band Rule.** The closing CTA band is teal #124D5C with white text in both themes; in dark mode it does not follow the mint brand swap. Its buttons invert: a white fill with teal text, and a white-outline ghost.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Inter (with system-ui, -apple-system, Segoe UI, sans-serif), variable opsz 14-32, weights 400-700, features cv11 and ss01.
**Body Font:** Inter.
**Label/Mono Font:** JetBrains Mono (with ui-monospace, SFMono-Regular, Menlo), weights 400/500/600.

**Character:** One neutral, precise grotesque doing all the talking at tight negative tracking, with a monospace that only appears when the text is literally something you type or a place in the code.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (700, clamp 36-64px, 1.04, -0.035em): the hero headline only; max width 14.5em, two lines on desktop; 34px on phones. Legal page titles use a smaller step (clamp 32-44px, -0.03em).
- **Headline** (700, clamp 30-44px, 1.1, -0.03em): section heads, left-aligned or centered; the closing band uses clamp 28-40px.
- **Title** (700, 17-20px, -0.01 to -0.02em): card, step, column and plan heads. 17px in cards, 19-20px in step and column headers.
- **Lede** (400, clamp 17-20px, 1.55): hero subcopy in ink-2, max 36em. Section intros are 18px ink-2.
- **Body** (400, 17px, 1.6): running text; FAQ answers cap at 62ch. Secondary descriptions run 15-16px in ink-2.
- **Label** (600, 14-15px, line-height 1): buttons, nav, form labels, FAQ summaries (17px).
- **Mono** (400-600, 11.5-13.5px): terminal pane, commands, file paths, BR/AC identifiers, citation sources. Inline code in prose is 0.86em on a sunk chip.
- **Price** (700, 46px, -0.035em, tabular numerals): plan prices only.
- **Small steps** (11-14.5px): caption 14px and 14.5px, caption-sm 13.5px, meta 12.5px, pill 12px. The two smallest, micro-label (11.5px, uppercase, 0.08em) and tag (11px), are for the product rendering only: terminal speaker labels, ticket section labels and the ticket status tag. Page text never goes below 12px.
- **Font stacks:** the full Inter stack is on body, display and headings. Buttons use `"Inter", system-ui, sans-serif` and small rendering labels use `"Inter", sans-serif`. Mono uses `"JetBrains Mono", ui-monospace, monospace` for terminal text, `"JetBrains Mono", monospace` for paths, IDs and command chips, and the full stack for inline code.

### Named Rules
**The Mono Means Literal Rule.** JetBrains Mono is reserved for real commands, file paths, `file:line` citations and ticket IDs. Never use it for headings, labels or flavor.

**The Tight Display Rule.** Headings tighten as they grow: -0.01em at title size, -0.03em at headline, -0.035em at display. Body text keeps default tracking.

## Layout

A centered 1160px container with 24px gutters (16px under 640px). Sections are separated by 120px of space (88px under 900px), with a 48px gap between a section head (max 40em) and its content. The hero is centered: headline, one-sentence lede, two actions side by side, and a single fine-print row of three checked facts; the product window starts inside the first viewport, 44px below the actions, at up to 1120px wide.

Content grids are two-column (equal, or a 1.25:1 split when one item leads and spans two rows) with a 20px gap; the three-step sequence uses hairline column dividers instead of cards. Pricing pairs two plans in a 920px column. Every multi-column grid collapses to one column at 900px; the steps become stacked rows divided by top hairlines; on phones the closing band goes full-bleed and square-cornered, and the tracker table turns into labelled stacked rows.

The header is sticky (64px), translucent page color with a 12px backdrop blur, and gains a hairline bottom border only after scrolling. Secondary nav links hide under 640px, leaving brand and the "Get Pro" button.

## Elevation & Depth

A hybrid of hairlines and soft shadows. Every raised surface has a 1px border first; shadows are diffuse, teal-tinted and low-opacity, and add lift rather than structure. There are two levels. Dark mode swaps the tint for black at higher opacity.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Lifted** (`0 1px 2px rgba(14,31,36,.06), 0 12px 32px -8px rgba(14,31,36,.18), 0 40px 80px -24px rgba(14,31,36,.22)`): the product window and the Pro plan (with a 1px brand ring). The hero object of the page.
- **Resting** (`0 1px 2px rgba(14,31,36,.06), 0 4px 12px -4px rgba(14,31,36,.10)`): proof fragments and the tracker table.
- **Button bevel** (`0 1px 2px rgba(14,31,36,.2), inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255,255,255,.12)`): primary buttons only.

### Named Rules
**The Border Before Shadow Rule.** No surface floats on shadow alone; a hairline border defines the edge and the shadow only lifts it. Hard, unblurred offset shadows are not part of this system.

## Shapes

Gently rounded throughout, stepping up with the size of the object: 5px inline code, 6px the focus outline, 7px the brand icon, 8px nav links, command chips and the hold callout, 10px buttons and inputs, 12px cards, 16px the product window and pricing plans, 20px the closing band. Pills (999px) are reserved for small status tags. Round 28px step numerals and 36px rounded-square (9px) icon tiles use mint tint. Window dots and numerals are circles (50%); the closing band loses its corners (0) when it goes full-bleed on phones. The product window shows a 40px chrome bar with three neutral dots and a centered title.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Shape:** gently rounded (10px), 44px tall, 50px for the large size used in hero, pricing and closing band; 38px compact in the header.
- **Primary:** teal fill, white 600 15px label, subtle bevel shadow; hover deepens to brand-teal-hover; active nudges down 1px. In dark mode: mint fill with dark ink.
- **Secondary:** surface fill, ink label, strong-hairline border; hover darkens the border to ink-3.
- **On-band:** inside the closing band, a white fill with teal text, and a transparent ghost with a 45% white outline.
- **Focus:** 2px focus-teal outline at 3px offset. Disabled: 50% opacity.

### Chips and Tags
- **Status pill:** mint tint fill, mint-ink 600 11-12px text, fully rounded ("Ready for dev", "One-time").
- **Verdict / hold:** hold tint with hold orange text, for "Blocked" and "decision needed" only.
- **Command chip:** mono 13px on sunk page, hairline border, 8px radius, used for the command a step runs.

### Cards / Containers
- **Corner Style:** 12px (fragments, path columns, table), 16px (plans, window).
- **Background:** surface; header rows and table heads use sunk page.
- **Shadow Strategy:** Resting for proof fragments and the table, Lifted for the window and Pro plan (see Elevation).
- **Border:** 1px hairline; the Pro plan uses a brand-colored border plus ring.
- **Internal Padding:** 20-30px headers and bodies, 32px plans (24px on phones).

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** 46px tall, 10px radius, strong-hairline border, page-colored fill, an inline muted "@" prefix.
- **Focus:** border turns focus teal with a 3px focus ring at 25% opacity.
- **Error:** border turns hold orange; the note below switches to hold orange. Success note: ink on mint tint, 10px radius.

### Navigation
- Brand lockup (26px icon at 7px radius, 650 17px wordmark), then ink-2 15px 500 links with 8px-radius sunk hover, then a compact primary button. Footer repeats the lockup with ink-2 links that underline on hover.

### Product Window (signature)
A faithful rendering of ReadyGate in Claude Code: chrome bar, then two panes (1.08fr : 1fr, stacked under 900px). Left: the terminal on terminal night in mono 13.5px, with muted uppercase speaker labels, white commands with mint command names, ref-blue citations, an orange-washed hold callout, and a mint success line. Right: a ticket on warm paper with a mono file path, a status pill, a 19px title, small uppercase tracked section labels and teal mono BR/AC IDs. The uppercase labels belong to the rendered product, not to the page's own typography.

### Proof Fragment (signature)
A card with a title and one-line description in its header, ending in a slice of real output: either a terminal strip (term background, peach "Blocked." label, ref-blue paths) or a paper strip with a teal AC ID. The leading fragment spans two rows with larger output text.

### Closing Band
Full-width teal band, 20px radius, centered headline and copy at 84% white, on-band buttons. Fixed teal in both themes.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** let renderings of the real product carry the visual weight: terminal panes, ticket paper, real commands and `file:line` citations.
- **Do** use teal for every primary action and link, and swap the brand role to mint in dark mode.
- **Do** pair every raised surface with a 1px hairline before adding the Resting or Lifted shadow.
- **Do** reserve hold orange for "decision needed" and "Blocked" states and their form-validation echo.
- **Do** keep the window settle (1.1s, cubic-bezier(.16, 1, .3, 1), transform only) as the single entrance animation, disabled under prefers-reduced-motion.
- **Do** collapse every grid to one column at 900px and keep 16px gutters on phones.

### Don't:
- **Don't** use warm paper anywhere but inside a rendered ticket.
- **Don't** set headings, labels or flavor text in JetBrains Mono.
- **Don't** add gradient blobs, illustrations or icon-card grids in place of product renderings.
- **Don't** introduce a second accent hue or use orange decoratively.
- **Don't** use hard, unblurred offset shadows.
- **Don't** put small uppercase tracked labels above page headings; that treatment lives only inside the product rendering.
